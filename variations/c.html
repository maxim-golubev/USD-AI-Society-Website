Editorial

What we believe

We believe AI is going to show up in every job, not only the technical ones. Accountants, marketers and nurses will all work alongside it, and the people who learn early will have an easier time.

We believe you learn it by building something you'll actually use. A demo you forget by Friday doesn't count.

We believe using AI well includes knowing where it fails. We'd rather you leave skeptical and skilled than excited and careless.

And we believe the best ideas come from mixing people. A finance major and a philosophy major should be building in the same room.

What we do

Workshops where you build.

Every workshop ends with something on your laptop that works. You'll understand it because you made it.

People who use AI at work.

We bring in people from companies using AI right now, so you hear what's working for them and what isn't.

Real projects.

We're starting to work with local companies on real problems, which means experience you can actually talk about in an interview.

Honest about the limits.

We talk about what AI gets wrong and how to use it responsibly. That part matters as much as the rest.

How we started

The AI Society grew out of Dr. Alan Lerchbacker's classes at Knauss in spring 2026. A group of his students wanted a place at USD to actually learn AI instead of just reading headlines about it, so we started one.

By May we had a board. In September we set up a table at the Alcalá Bazaar and more than fifty students signed up. Our first workshop is on October 20.

Work with us

If you work somewhere that's figuring out AI, we'd love to meet you. We're business students from every major learning to use AI well, and we learn best from people doing it for real.

We're new, so the people who work with us early help decide what this becomes.
usdaisociety@gmail.com

  1. Speak at a meeting.

    Twenty minutes on how your team actually uses AI goes a long way with us.

  2. Run a workshop.

    Show students the tools your industry uses day to day.

  3. Bring us a problem.

    Give a team of students a real question from your business. You get fresh eyes and honest work.

  4. Meet future hires.

    Our members are business students who know how to work with AI. We'd love to introduce you.
