Worth reading

This week
I.

A bank that sorts 120,000 emails a day with AI.

Barclays is expanding its use of Claude across the bank. It already sorts about 120,000 client emails a day and helps 16,000 employees find answers for customers, and it wants most of its software engineers using it by 2027. A real look at AI inside a finance company.

Anthropic →
II.

$100 million to train the people who bring AI into companies.

Anthropic launched the Claude Frontier Academy to train 10,000 engineers by the end of 2027 who help businesses adopt AI. Accenture, Bain, Deloitte, McKinsey and Morgan Stanley are among the first to send people. The person who makes AI work at a company is turning into its own job.

Anthropic →
III.

The people building AI, testifying about it.

Executives from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta appeared at a New York City Council hearing on AI this week, where one researcher warned, “we are racing to build and grow our own adversary.” Worth reading for both sides of the argument.
